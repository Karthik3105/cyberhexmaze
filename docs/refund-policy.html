Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

HoneyMaze is developed, owned, and operated by Karthik G G. We want our players to have the best experience while playing HoneyMaze. This policy outlines how refunds and cancellations are handled for digital in-app purchases.

1. In-App Purchases (Digital Content)

HoneyMaze offers optional virtual in-app purchases, including hints, puzzle items, and ad-free upgrades. All in-app purchase transactions are processed securely through the Google Play Store billing platform.

2. Refund Eligibility & Process

Because in-app items are digital goods delivered instantly upon purchase:

  • Within 48 hours of purchase: You can request a refund directly through Google Play by following the instructions on the Google Play Refund Request page.
  • Technical issues or non-delivery: If an in-app purchase failed to deliver your virtual items due to a technical glitch, or if you were charged multiple times for a single order, please contact our support team with your Google Play Order Transaction ID (GPA.XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXXX).

3. Cancellation Policy

Consumable in-app items (such as hint packs or coins) cannot be cancelled once consumed in the game. Subscriptions (if any) can be cancelled at any time by visiting Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & Subscriptions → Subscriptions.
